Human models are replacing animal models worldwide, but they are not yet reproducible: the same protocol gives different results in different laboratories. Now is the time to invest. The DECRA paid the cost of building the lab from scratch, and its outputs, trained people and network are landing now.

Evidence. Same two stem-cell lines, one protocol, five laboratories: the laboratory was the largest source of variation, and only 15 differentially expressed genes were shared by all five (Volpato et al. 2018).

Before 1841, every workshop cut its own screw threads. Joseph Whitworth standardised the thread and built a machine that measured to a millionth of an inch. Interchangeable parts and mass production followed.

Today, every laboratory's differentiation protocol fits only its own laboratory. We are building the thread gauge.
