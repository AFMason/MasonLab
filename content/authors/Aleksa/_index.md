Aleksa is an HDR Candidate using synthetic cell models — droplet interface bilayers (DIBs) and droplet hydrogel bilayers (DHBs) — to study lipid membranes, pore-forming proteins, and DNA nanopores. They visualise these events using various forms of microscopy, including single-molecule TIRF microscopy, aiming to show that random fluctuations at the molecular level can be productive for biological activity.

They were drawn to the project by an interest in making science easier and more reproducible: the lab's DIB-BOT liquid handling robot opened up many potential optimisations for DIB and DHB workflows.

Outside the lab, Aleksa enjoys reading, movies, tennis, and Python — a self-proclaimed pythonista.
